Define the Fourier series

Complex exponential Fourier series:

\[\widetilde{x}_N(t)=\sum_{k=-N}^{N}a_k e^{j\frac{2\pi}{T}kt}\]

Sawtooth wave: choose Custom to enter your own Fourier coefficients.

Use formulas in k for positive and negative integer indices. For a real-valued signal, choose coefficients with \(a_{-k}=a_k^*\).

For an odd number M, enter all M coefficients from \(a_{-(M-1)/2}\) to \(a_{(M-1)/2}\).

Expression tips
Use k (positive or negative), pi, sin(), cos(), abs(), sqrt(), and ^ for powers. For example, -2/(pi*k)*sin(pi*k/2)^2 for the imaginary part of square-wave coefficients. The graphs support complex-valued sums. For real-valued signals, use conjugate-symmetric coefficients a(-k) = conjugate(a(k)). Example: for a(k) = -j/(k^2+2), enter 0 for the real part and -1/(k^2+2) for the imaginary part.
